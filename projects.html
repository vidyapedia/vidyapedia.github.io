Pressure-Washing Drone

An ongoing aerial cleaning platform integrating multirotor flight hardware, fluid delivery, washer actuation, Raspberry Pi computing, Pixhawk flight control, camera sensing, and LiDAR development.

Pixhawk Raspberry Pi LiDAR

ROS 2 Autonomous Patrol Robot

An indoor patrol system using ROS 2 Humble, Nav2, AMCL, occupancy-grid maps, waypoint orchestration, recovery behavior, obstacle avoidance, dynamic replanning, and navigation performance logging.

ROS 2 Nav2 AMCL

Odyssey Mars Rover

A multidisciplinary rover developed for the Indian Space Research Organisation Robotics Challenge, combining terrain mobility, perception, manipulator control, embedded electronics, power distribution, and subsystem integration.

ROS 2 Jetson Nano System Integration

Servo-Based Quadruped Robot

An eight-servo physical quadruped platform developed for mechanical prototyping, Raspberry Pi control, custom CAD, 3D-printed components, gait sequencing, and hardware stability experiments.

Raspberry Pi LX-16A Gait Control

Physics-Informed Brain MRI Registration

A computational research pipeline for longitudinal 3D brain MRI registration using segmentation masks, signed distance fields, Neural ODE transformations, physics-informed correction, regional labels, and Dice-score evaluation.
