A little about myself

Electrical roots. Mechanical systems. One integrated view.

My background spans electronics and communication engineering, mechanical engineering, robotics, controls, sensors, and embedded computing. That cross-disciplinary path is the reason I’m most at home at system boundaries—where perception, hardware, software, power, and actuation have to work together.

I care about the unglamorous parts that make robots real: calibration, interfaces, safe failure behavior, test plans, power limits, iteration, and evidence.
