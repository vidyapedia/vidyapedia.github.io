My engineering path

From circuits to complete robotic systems.

I began in electronics and communication engineering, where I learned to think in sensors, interfaces, embedded computing, and signals. At Columbia, I expanded that foundation into mechanical systems, robotics, controls, MEMS, and autonomy.

Today, my strongest work sits across the whole robot stack. I’m comfortable moving from a camera or transducer, through embedded software and state estimation, into control logic, motors, mechanisms, power, and system-level testing.

That makes me especially interested in robotics and mechatronics integration, validation, controls, sensing, and physical-AI roles—work where reliability depends on understanding the entire system rather than one isolated component.
